Raised on sound systems

DJ Slender, Slendz to most, grew up around London sound systems. His dad and uncles ran rigs playing rare groove, soul and lovers rock, one uncle was a well-known calypso artist, and cousins went on to DJ house, hip hop, soca and dancehall.

He first touched the decks at 13. Since 2008 he's taken that education from house parties to clubs, residencies at bars and restaurants across London, weddings, birthdays and high-profile corporate events in the UK and abroad. He also hosted a weekly R&B and slow jams show on Pulse88 radio.

Off the decks he's spent more than ten years as a professional AV engineer, so the sound at your event is in safe hands too.

He reads the crowd, not a playlist, and aims to exceed a client's expectations before they've had to ask.
